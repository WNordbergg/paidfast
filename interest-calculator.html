What rate applies to you?

European Union (commercial invoices)

Directive 2011/7/EU: statutory interest = European Central Bank main refinancing rate + 8 percentage points, plus at least €40 in recovery costs — you don't need a contract clause, and interest accrues automatically from the day after the due date. With the ECB rate around 2%, that's roughly ~10% p.a. Check the current ECB rate for the exact figure.

Sweden

Dröjsmålsränta enligt räntelagen = Riksbankens referensränta + 8 procentenheter. Referensräntan är 2,00 % för perioden 1 juli – 31 december 2026 (källa: Sveriges Riksbank) → 10,00 % just nu. Svenska versionen av kalkylatorn: dröjsmålsränta-kalkylator →

United States

No federal default rate — the interest you can charge comes from your contract first, then state law. If your invoice/terms state a monthly rate (commonly 1–1.5%/month), use that annualized. If nothing was agreed, courts may still award interest as damages — check your state's rules.

General information, not legal advice. Rates shown are editable defaults — always verify the current official rate for large claims.
