Late-payment questions, answered

What tone should I use for a first reminder?

Start friendly — most late payments are simple oversight. A warm nudge resolves the majority of cases without damaging the relationship. Escalate only if you get silence.

When should I send a final notice?

A common cadence: friendly reminder ~3–7 days after the due date, firm reminder ~14 days, final notice ~30 days with a concrete deadline and consequences. Always give one clear way to resolve it (pay, or reply with a payment date).

Can I charge late fees or interest?

Often yes, but rules vary. In the EU, commercial late-payment interest and recovery costs are generally allowed by directive 2011/7/EU. In Sweden, dröjsmålsränta plus a standard invoice fee applies by law. In the US, check your contract and state law. Mentioning legitimate fees in a reminder is usually enough to motivate payment.
